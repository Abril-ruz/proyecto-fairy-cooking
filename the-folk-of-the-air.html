The Folk of the Air

Elfhame is a place of ancient forests, glittering courts, and creatures unlike anything found in our world. And, of course, its food is no exception. Come with us beyond the pages of The Folk of the Air and discover what might await you at a Faerie feast.

In Holly Black's The Folk of the Air, food is more than something to be enjoyed. It belongs to a world of enchantment, temptation, and danger, where a beautiful feast can hide something far more sinister beneath the surface.

The Folk eat, drink, celebrate, and feast differently from mortals, with tables overflowing with strange fruits, decadent sweets, rich dishes, and enchanted delicacies. But for humans, there is one important rule to remember: never eat what the Folk offer you.
